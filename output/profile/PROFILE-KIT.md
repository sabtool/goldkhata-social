## Ten minutes of tapping, in this order

### 1 · Name  → Edit profile → Name

```
GoldKhata | Gold Loan Software
```
30 characters exactly. This is the field Instagram search matches, and right now
it holds only a name. Instagram allows two name changes in 14 days, so set it once.

### 2 · Bio  → Edit profile → Bio

```
For gold loan firms: one place for loans, payments and notices.
Interest follows your own method, not a textbook.
30 days free, no card 👇
```
138 of 150 characters. Line one says who it is for and what changes; line two is
the thing no other software says. No price, no claim we cannot prove.

### 3 · Link  → Edit profile → Links

```
goldkhata.com/trial?utm_source=instagram
```
One door, matching what the bio just promised. The tag tells you how many trials
Instagram actually sends you.

### 4 · Category and contact  → Edit profile

- Category: **Software company**
- Contact: an email you read, and the call button on +91 88048 88883

### 5 · Pin three posts  → on each post, ⋯ → Pin to your profile

| Slot | Post | Job it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **19 Sep** — carousel, "I already paid last month" | the proof |
| 2 | **2 Oct** — "30 days free. We move your records for you." | the offer |
| 3 | **21 Sep** — "Your business. Your rules." (interest) | who we are |

Pin all three, then look at the row. If the order is wrong, unpin the one you want
first and pin it again last.

(Copy the six cover files to your phone first.)
